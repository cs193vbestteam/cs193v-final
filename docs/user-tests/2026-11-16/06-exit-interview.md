# Exit interview

From the user test on November 16, 2026. Who took part and how it was run: [README](README.md).

The answers are the tester's.

**Who do you think can see what you type into the system?**

> "Based on how the website presents itself, probably only the AI can see it. Other people shouldn't be able to see it."

**How do you think the restaurant recommendations were chosen?**

> "Probably based on my preferences and the other user's preferences, choosing restaurants that fit our requirements and the date."

**Would you enter your real budget? Why or why not?**

> "Yes. Nobody else would know, so why not give the real number?"

**Would you use this instead of a group chat?**

> "Maybe. There's a lot to talk through on the website, and it feels a bit complicated. If we really couldn't decide in the group chat, we might use it as a last resort."

**If only one thing could change, what would you choose?**

> "The chat page has too much going on. There isn't a clear focus, so it's hard to know where to look or what to click. It needs to make the next step more obvious."

The tester also asked again why registration was necessary and suggested allowing guests.
