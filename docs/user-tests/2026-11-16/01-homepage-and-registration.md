# Homepage and registration

From the user test on November 16, 2026. Who took part and how it was run: [README](README.md).

**Completed:** Yes.

**Path:** Browse homepage → "Start a plan" → Sign up → Create account.

The tester browsed the homepage and liked the animation on the right. They said it was done very well.

They clicked "Start a plan," then discovered that an account was required. They felt that registering for this kind of tool was inconvenient.

The tester entered a very simple password, and registration succeeded. This surprised the observer. The site's only rule is that a password has at least 8 characters, so a simple one of that length is accepted.

The tester raised the registration issue again during the exit interview:

> "Why does this website need registration? Why can't people just join as guests?"
