# The result

From the user test on November 16, 2026. Who took part and how it was run: [README](README.md).

**Completed:** Yes, but the result was easy to miss.

**Path:** Look for completion feedback → Notice result in lower-right area → Review options → "Open in Google Maps."

There was confusion about whether the five-person limit meant five people were needed. A result did appear with two participants, but the tester did not notice it immediately.

The recommendation appeared in a small area toward the lower right. The tester felt it should have its own page because it was the main outcome of the process.

The "Not done yet" button remained visible, which added to the uncertainty about whether the process was finished.

The second participant noticed that the restaurant they had requested appeared near the top of the recommendations. They reacted positively to this.

They selected "Open in Google Maps." It opened successfully, which they also liked.

The tester felt that the six restaurant options were too text-heavy and looked a little unattractive. They suggested adding restaurant pictures or icons. However, they also said the simple appearance was acceptable because the website had a minimal style overall.

**Moments worth noticing:**

- The result did not draw enough attention when it appeared.
