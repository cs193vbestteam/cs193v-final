# Finishing and inviting

From the user test on November 16, 2026. Who took part and how it was run: [README](README.md).

**Completed:** Yes, after searching for the controls.

**Path:** Finish answering → Find "I'm done" → Click it → Search for invitation control → "Copy invite link" → Send link.

The tester said the "I'm done" button was too far down the page and did not fit the natural flow of the interaction.

After clicking it, the button changed to "Not done yet." The tester did not understand what this meant.

They suggested showing a waiting or progress state that made the next step clearer. For example, the page could tell them that their part was finished and that they could invite someone else.

The tester then searched for a way to invite another person and found "Copy invite link."

**Moments worth noticing:**

- The tester had to look for the completion button.
- "Not done yet" was not understood as a way to undo completion.
- After finishing, the tester did not immediately know what to do next.
