# Seed: a Stanford friend group trying to get dinner

Five friends from a CS class at Stanford have been trying to get dinner together for
two weeks. Every attempt dies in the group chat: someone says "whatever works", someone
sends a link nobody opens, someone is quietly broke this month, someone lives off
campus and hates driving, someone is vegetarian but never brings it up because it feels
like a hassle. They would all genuinely like to eat together this weekend (Friday night
through Sunday night). Budgets, free time, how far they'll travel and what they won't eat
are real and different for each of them, and nobody wants to say the numbers out loud.
